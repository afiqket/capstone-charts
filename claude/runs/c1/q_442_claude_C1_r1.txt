## Answer

The average vacation days per year across all 14 countries in the data increased by **20 days** — from about **13.8 days in 1870** to about **33.8 days in 2000**.

### Details

| Year | Sum across 14 countries | Average |
|------|------------------------|---------|
| 1870 | 193 | 193 ÷ 14 ≈ **13.79** |
| 2000 | 473 | 473 ÷ 14 ≈ **33.79** |

**Increase = (473 − 193) ÷ 14 = 280 ÷ 14 = exactly 20 days.**

A note on scope: the chart itself only *plots* 5 countries (US, UK, Netherlands, France, Germany), but the data file contains 14 countries (Australia, Belgium, Canada, Denmark, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Netherlands, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, UK, US). I computed the average "across all countries" using all 14 present in both 1870 and 2000.
