When you hover over the Ages 60–79 area near 2000, the chart's interaction handler snaps to the nearest year (2000) and:

1. **Draws a vertical hover line** (`#888`, 1px) at x = 2000 spanning the full chart height.
2. **Shows a tooltip** positioned next to the cursor. Note: the hover region is one big invisible `<rect>` over the whole plot area — it's not bound to the Ages 60–79 layer specifically, so hovering anywhere at that x-position shows the same tooltip.
