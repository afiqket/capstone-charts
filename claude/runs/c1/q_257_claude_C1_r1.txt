An upward line for the United States indicates that the **share of the U.S. population born in another country is increasing over time** — i.e., immigrants make up a growing percentage of the total U.S. population in the years where the line slopes up.

Per the chart's subtitle ("Immigrants as a share of the total population") and the value column `"Share of the population born in another country"`, the y-axis is the foreign-born percentage of the population. A rising line therefore reflects growth in the foreign-born share, which can result from higher immigration inflows, lower emigration, and/or slower growth of the native-born population — not necessarily an increase in the absolute number of immigrants alone.
