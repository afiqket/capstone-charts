Yes — this chart has a built-in bar chart mode. Here's how to switch to it:

## How to view the bar chart

At the top-left of the chart there are two toggle buttons (chart_144.js:45-46):

- **📈 Line** (active by default)
- **📊 Bar**

**Click the "📊 Bar" button** and the chart re-renders as a horizontal bar chart (chart_144.js:276-277 → `setMode("bar")` → `renderBar()`).

## What you'll see in bar mode

- The six rights become **horizontal bars**, sorted largest-to-smallest, all in a single blue-gray color (`#6b7fad`), with the country count labeled at the end of each bar (chart_144.js:167-211).
- The chart shows **one year at a time**. The title updates to include the year, e.g. *"…World, 2023"*.

## Picking the year

Bar mode reveals a **year slider** near the bottom (hidden in line mode, shown in bar mode — chart_144.js:271). To choose which year's data the bars show, you can:

1. **Drag the round handle** along the slider (chart_144.js:248-251), or
2. **Click anywhere on the slider track** to jump to the nearest year (chart_144.js:243-246), or
3. **Click the ▶ play button** on the left to animate through every year from 1970 → 2023 automatically (~120 ms per year), watching the bars grow (chart_144.js:253-264).

It defaults to the latest year, **2023**, where the World values are: Equal right to start a business 184, Equal property rights 170, Employment discrimination prohibited 161, Domestic violence sanctioned 164, Substantial paid leave for mothers 122, Pay equity mandated 97.

To go back, just click **📈 Line** again.
