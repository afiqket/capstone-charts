**Neither** is the most stable category for All adults.

Across the eight survey waves (Dec 2020 – Jun 2024), "Neither" stays within a 33–35% band (33, 33, 35, 33, 33, 34, 33, 34), a range of just 2 percentage points. Every other category swings more: Positive 17→28, Negative 26→34, Not applicable 5→12, Don't know 4→9.
