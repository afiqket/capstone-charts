When you hover over 2020 on the line chart, two things happen:

**1. A vertical hover line** (gray, 1px) appears at the 2020 x-position spanning the full chart height.

**2. A tooltip** appears near the cursor showing:
- **Title:** `2020`
- **Rows** (sorted by value descending, each with a colored swatch):
  - 🟦 Mumbai: 26,696 per km²
  - 🟫 Karachi: 24,328 per km²
  - 🟪 Surat: 23,384 per km²
  - 🟩 Kinshasa: 21,869 per km²
  - 🟦 Hong Kong: 21,763 per km²
  - 🟥 Kabul: 15,289 per km²

None of the rows carry a `(proj.)` suffix — 2020 is the cutoff, and the projection filter only includes years strictly greater than 2020 (line 30), so all six values come from the estimated column.

The tooltip is positioned to the right of the cursor unless that would overflow the chart, in which case it flips to the left.
