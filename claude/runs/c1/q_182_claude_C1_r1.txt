Syria shows a **non-monotonic** trend overall, but with two clear phases:

- **1989–2010**: Flat at 0 deaths.
- **2011–2017**: Sharp **increase**, rising from 30 to a peak of 12,464 in 2017 (with a dip in 2015).
- **2017–2021**: Steep **decrease**, falling to 307 by 2021.
- **2022–2024**: Roughly stable at a few hundred per year.

So there is no single trend across the years — Syria rises sharply through the mid-2010s (Syrian civil war) and then **decreases** significantly afterward. The overall arc since 2011 is an increase followed by a larger decrease.
