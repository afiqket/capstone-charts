**Synthetic Cannabinoid Cases** had more total cases over the whole period.

Summing every month in the data:
- **Synthetic Cannabinoid: ≈ 17,960 cases** — driven by very high early counts in 2015 (e.g. 1,511 in April and 1,205 in May), then a long decline.
- **CBD: ≈ 12,150 cases** — near zero through 2015–2017, then climbing steeply to 500–630/month by 2021–2022.

So despite CBD overtaking synthetics on a monthly basis from mid-2019 onward, the synthetic cannabinoid category still wins on cumulative total, mostly because of its huge 2015–2017 volume.
