When you hover over the United States bar, the **`showTooltip` handler fires** (`mouseenter`/`mousemove`), and a tooltip appears. The bar itself does **not** change — there's no color, opacity, or stroke change on hover, only the tooltip is shown/positioned.

**What the tooltip displays** (pulled from the data row for United States):

| | |
|---|---|
| **Title (dark header)** | United States |
| Revenue Share | 45% |
| Profit Margin | 17% |
| Economy | U.S. |

**Where it appears:**

The tooltip is centered horizontally on the bar's end (at `x(margin)` ≈ the 17% mark), clamped to stay inside the plot area.

Vertically, it tries to sit *below* the bar's center (`barCenterY + 22`). But because the U.S. bar is the tallest (45% of revenue → ~159px) and sits low in the stack, placing the tooltip below would overflow the bottom of the chart. So the code takes the flip branch and instead positions the tooltip **above** the bar's center, with the little triangle **arrow pointing downward** toward the bar.

The tooltip is also `.raise()`d to the front, and disappears again on `mouseleave`.

One detail worth noting: for the U.S. row the "Economy" value is literally `"U.S."` (that's its category), so the tooltip's Economy line reads **U.S.**, matching the pink legend color used for the bar.
