The darker pink/purple color on the map represents a **higher share of people who say science helps "a lot" in treating anxiety or depression**.

The color scale is sequential, running from light pink (`#fff0f6`) at 0% up through dark magenta/purple (`#4a0030`) at 50%. So the darker the pink/purple, the larger the percentage of respondents in that country who believe science is very helpful for treating anxiety or depression (e.g., countries like Senegal ~51.8%, Philippines ~50.6%, Malta ~54.4%, Ethiopia ~48.6% would appear in the darkest shades).
