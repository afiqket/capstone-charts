The slider controls the **time** being visualized on the flight-tracking map. Specifically:

- Its range is set to the min/max timestamps found in the flight data (`slider.min = minTime; slider.max = maxTime`), with a step of 40 seconds.
- When the user drags it, the `input` event fires `updateForTime(event.target.value)`, which:
  1. Updates `currentTime` to the slider's value.
  2. Updates the text label next to the slider to show the corresponding UTC timestamp (`formatTime`).
  3. Recomputes which flights are airborne at that moment and their interpolated positions (`recomputeActivePoints`).
  4. Redraws the dynamic layers — the blue flight trails leading up to that time and the red dots marking each plane's current position.

In short, the slider scrubs through the two hours of OpenSky flight data on September 3rd 2019, animating each plane's location and trailing path at the selected moment.
