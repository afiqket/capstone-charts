To see the migration paths for Lincoln, ID on this chart, **click on Lincoln County in Idaho on the map**.

Here's what happens (from `chart_054.js`):

1. Each county feature has a `properties.flows` object — a map of `targetCountyId → netMigrationValue` (`drawFlows`, line 150).
2. The click handler on `countyLayer` (lines 217–223) highlights the clicked county yellow (`#f2c94c`) and calls `drawFlows(d)` for it.
3. `drawFlows` draws a curved arc from Lincoln's centroid to every other county that appears in its `flows` map (lines 153–203):
   - **Positive value → net gain** for Lincoln, colored on the blue→green ramp (`gainColors`).
   - **Negative value → net loss** from Lincoln, colored on the yellow→red ramp (`lossColors`).
4. You can hover over a county to see its name in the tooltip (helpful since Idaho has a "Lincoln County" — make sure you pick the one in ID, not in other states with the same name). Use scroll/drag to zoom into Idaho first if needed (zoom is wired up at lines 225–235).

Note: clicking only draws Lincoln's outgoing arc set. The chart does not separately render arcs *from other counties into* Lincoln — every arc shown when Lincoln is selected represents a flow recorded under Lincoln's own `flows` entry (positive = inbound to Lincoln, negative = outbound from Lincoln, based on the legend "Net gain / Net loss").
