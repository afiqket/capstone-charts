Moving the slider in **line** mode controls the **start year of the chart's x-axis window** — it doesn't mark a single year the way it does on the map/bar views.

Here's the exact chain. Dragging the handle (or clicking the track) runs:

```js
currentYear = clamp(round(sliderScale.invert(event.x)))
redraw()   // → renderLine() since mode === "line"
```

And the first line of `renderLine()` is the key:

```js
const lineStartYear = currentYear >= maxYear ? minYear : currentYear;
```

So the slider position becomes the **left edge** of the time range, while the right edge is always `maxYear` (2024). Concretely:

- **Slider at the far left (1989 = `minYear`)** → `lineStartYear` = 1989 → full range 1989–2024 shown.
- **Slider in the middle (e.g. 2010)** → `lineStartYear` = 2010 → chart zooms to just 2010–2024.
- **Slider at the far right (2024 = `maxYear`)** → the `>= maxYear` branch fires and it snaps **back to the full range** 1989–2024 (not an empty 2024-only window).

So as you slide right, the chart progressively "trims" earlier years and zooms into the recent period — until you hit the very end, where it resets to showing everything.

Each move triggers a full re-render of the line view, which also:
- **re-filters** each country's series to `Year >= lineStartYear` (`seriesData`),
- **rescales both axes** — the x-domain becomes `[lineStartYear, maxYear]`, and the y-domain is recomputed (`.nice()`) from only the *visible* values' max, so the vertical scale can jump as you zoom,
- recomputes the x-tick spacing and the right-edge country labels (with their anti-overlap nudging),
- **stops any running play animation** (`stopPlay()`), since dragging counts as manual control.

One thing it does *not* do: in line mode the slider position is **not** drawn as a vertical marker on the plot. The dashed vertical line + per-country dots only appear when you hover over the plot area, independent of the slider. (The blue "active track" still fills up to the handle on the slider itself.)

So in short: the bottom slider is effectively a **"zoom from year X to present"** control for the line chart, with the far-right position acting as a "show all years" reset.
