**Step 2 — Set the year to 2007 with the slider.** A slider appears at the bottom, running from 1990 (left) to 2015 (right). Move the year to 2007 in any of these ways:
- **Drag** the gray circular handle until it snaps to 2007, or
- **Click** the slider track at the spot for 2007 (it snaps to the nearest year), or
- Press the **▶ play** button to animate year-by-year and **⏸** pause on 2007.

The chart title updates to *"Emissions of air pollutants, France, 2007"*, and the bars redraw for that year (`renderBar()` uses `currentYear`).
