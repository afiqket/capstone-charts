## What the play button does

The play button is the small circular "▶" control sitting just left of the year slider handle. Its click handler:

```js
playBtn.on("click",()=>{
  if(playing){stopPlay();}else{
    playing=true; playIcon.text("⏸");
    if(currentYear>=maxYear) currentYear=minYear;
    playInterval=setInterval(()=>{
      currentYear=Math.min(currentYear+1,maxYear); redraw();
      if(currentYear>=maxYear) stopPlay();
    },350);
  }
});
```

So clicking it **starts an animated time-lapse**:

1. **Toggle behavior** — If an animation is already running, the click instead stops it (`stopPlay()`: clears the timer, resets the icon back to "▶"). Otherwise it starts one.

2. **On start:**
   - `playing` is set true and the icon changes from **▶ to ⏸** (pause symbol).
   - If `currentYear` is already at the end (`>= maxYear`), it rewinds to `minYear` first, so pressing play at the end replays from the beginning.

3. **The animation loop** — every **350 ms**, `currentYear` advances by 1 (capped at `maxYear`) and `redraw()` is called. `redraw()`:
   - moves the slider handle and blue active track to the new year (`updateSlider()`), and
   - re-renders whichever view is active — Map (default), Line, Slope, or Bar — for the new year. The dynamic title updates too (e.g. "Share of people in range of 4G mobile network, 2017").

4. **Auto-stop** — When `currentYear` reaches `maxYear`, `stopPlay()` fires: the interval is cleared, `playing` becomes false, and the icon returns to **▶**.

### Concretely with this data

The years span **2012 → 2024** (`minYear` = 2012, `maxYear` = 2024, taken from all distinct years in the CSV). The chart initializes at `currentYear = minYear` (2012).

So pressing play from the default state animates the map (or current view) **year-by-year from 2012 through 2024**, one step every 0.35 s — roughly a **~4-second** sweep — with countries/regions progressively filling in deeper blues as 4G coverage grows, then it stops automatically at 2024 with the icon reverting to ▶.

Two side notes:
- Dragging the handle or clicking the slider track calls `stopPlay()` first, so any manual interaction interrupts a running animation.
- For map fill, years with no exact row fall back to the most recent prior year's value (`getValueForYear`), so early animation frames (e.g. 2012–2014) will show many countries as "no data" hatching until their first data year appears.
