- **Central & East Africa:** DR Congo, Central African Republic, Congo, South Sudan, Sudan, Somalia, Ethiopia, Kenya, Burundi, Rwanda, Mozambique

This belt across the middle of Africa is the most solidly orange region on the map.

**2. The Middle East**

**3. South & Southeast Asia**
- Afghanistan, Pakistan, India, Myanmar, Thailand, Philippines, Indonesia, Bangladesh — a continuous orange stretch.

**In short:** in recent years the orange is concentrated in **Africa (especially the Sahel and Central/East Africa), the Middle East, and South/Southeast Asia** — the classic "conflict arc" — while the Americas (except Mexico/Colombia and a few others), Europe (except Russia/Ukraine), and the Asia‑Pacific developed states remain largely blue.
