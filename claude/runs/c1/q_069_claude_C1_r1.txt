Dragging the slider handle changes the **selected year**, which redraws the chart for that year. Here's the exact chain of behavior from the code:

The handle (the gray circle at `sliderG`) has a `d3.drag()` attached:

```js
handle.call(d3.drag().on("drag", function(event){
  stopPlay();
  currentYear = Math.max(minYear, Math.min(maxYear, Math.round(sliderScale.invert(event.x))));
  redraw();
}));
```

So on each drag movement:

1. **`stopPlay()`** — if the timeline animation is running (you pressed the ▶ play button), it's halted: the interval is cleared, `playing` is set to `false`, and the play icon resets from ⏸ back to ▶. Manual dragging always takes over from auto-play.

2. **`currentYear` is recomputed** — the drag x-position is converted to a year via `sliderScale.invert(event.x)`, rounded, and clamped to the data range **1989–2024** (`minYear`/`maxYear` come from the CSV's year set). The scale also has `.clamp(true)`, so dragging past either end just pins to 1989 or 2024.

3. **`redraw()`** runs, which does two things:
   - **`updateSlider()`** — moves the handle to the new year's position and stretches the blue "active track" line from the start up to the handle.
   - Re-renders whichever view is active:
     - **🌍 Map mode**: recolors every country by its death rate for that year (log color scale white→orange→dark red), and the title updates to `…, <year>`. Countries with no data stay gray/hatched.
     - **📊 Bar mode**: rebuilds the horizontal bar chart of the six selected countries (Syria, Afghanistan, Ethiopia, Eritrea, Iraq, Ukraine) for that year, re-sorted highest-to-lowest.
     - **📈 Line mode**: special case — the year you drag to becomes the **start year** of the x-axis (`lineStartYear = currentYear`), so dragging zooms/trims the time window of the line chart rather than picking a single point. (If the handle is at the max year, it resets to showing the full range from `minYear`.)

One detail: `getValueForYear` falls back to the most recent earlier year if a country has no row for the exact selected year (flagged as `estimated`), so map/bar values never go blank mid-range for countries that have data.

In short: **drag = scrub through time (1989–2024), stopping any running animation and live-updating the map/bars/line for the year under the handle.**
