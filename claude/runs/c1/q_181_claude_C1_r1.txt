Hovering over any colored region (each hex represents a UK area) triggers a tooltip that appears next to the cursor. The tooltip shows:

- The **region's name** (bold)
- The label "Walk three times a week"
- "2022:"
- The **percentage value** for that region, formatted to one decimal place (e.g., `42.3%`)

The tooltip follows the mouse as it moves within a region (via `mousemove`, repositioned ~15px right and 20px above the cursor), and disappears (`opacity: 0`) when the mouse leaves the region. So hovering across different regions one by one swaps the tooltip's content to match each hovered region — greener hexes will show lower percentages (closer to 30%), white hexes around 45%, and pink/magenta hexes higher values (toward 60%), matching the color scale.
