**2014 is far higher** — by a factor of roughly 400×.

A note on the data: the CSV's value column is `"Best estimate"`, which is a **count** of deaths from one-sided violence, not a per-capita rate (despite the tooltip labels in the code reading "deaths per 100,000 people" and the bar-chart title saying "Death rate"). The dataset contains no population figures, so the chart actually plots absolute death counts.

Across the full 2014–2024 span for Iraq, **2014 (3,205) is the peak year**, with 2016 next highest (2,403); values fall sharply after 2017, reaching single digits by 2022 (11) and 2024 (8).
