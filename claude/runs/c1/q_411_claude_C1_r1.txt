**The 1970s had the highest average difference between men and women — about 43.3 deaths per 100,000.**

This is the period where male rates had climbed to their peak plateau (~51–61) while female rates were still relatively low (~10–17), producing the widest gap. After ~1980 the gap narrows steadily as male rates fall and female rates keep rising.
