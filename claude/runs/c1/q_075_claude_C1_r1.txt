When you hover over a country shaded orange (`COLOR_YES = "#e8916a"`, meaning a war was ongoing that year), the `mousemove` handler on that country's path fires and does two things:

**1. Visual highlight on the map**
- All countries' borders are reset to thin white (`stroke #fff`, width `0.4`).
- The hovered country gets a dark outline (`stroke #333`, width `1.5`) and is raised to the front (`.raise()`), so it's visibly emphasized against its neighbors.

**2. A tooltip appears** (`showTooltip`)
A white rounded box (240×78, with a drop shadow) shows up near the cursor, containing three lines:
- **Country name** in bold (e.g., "Colombia").
- **The current year** in small gray text (whatever the slider is set to — `2010` by default, since `currentYear` starts at `maxYear`).
- Since the value is `1`, the result line reads **"Yes — conflict occurred"**, and that text is colored the same orange (`COLOR_YES`).

The tooltip flips to the left of the cursor if it would overflow the right edge, and is clamped vertically to stay on screen.

Concretely, with the default 2010 view, hovering an orange country like Colombia, Iraq, or Pakistan outlines it in dark gray and pops a tooltip such as:

> **Colombia**
> 2010
> **Yes — conflict occurred**  *(in orange)*

When you move off the country (`mouseleave`), the dark outline is removed and the tooltip is hidden. Note: this hover behavior only exists in **Map** mode — the Line (small-multiples) view has no tooltip.
