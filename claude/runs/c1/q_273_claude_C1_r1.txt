## How to find the highest birth order for 1990

The chart shows **South Korea** (filtered via `rows = rawData.filter(d => d.Entity === country)` with `country = "South Korea"`). There are two ways to find this in the chart:

**Option 1 — Use the Bar view (easiest):**
1. Click the **📊 Bar** button at the top.
2. Drag the slider at the bottom to **1990** (or click the play ▶ button until it reaches 1990).
3. The bars are sorted descending by value, so the **top bar is the highest**. Each bar is labeled and shows its numeric value.

**Option 2 — Use the Line view (hover):**
1. In the default Line view, hover over the chart around year 1990.
2. The tooltip shows all four series with their values for that year — pick the largest.

**Answer from the data (row for 1990):**

| Birth order | Value |
|---|---|
| First child | 108.6 |
| Second child | 117.2 |
| Third child | 190.8 |
| **Fourth child & higher** | **214.1** ← highest |

So in 1990, **"Fourth child & higher"** has the highest sex ratio at birth (214.1 males per 100 females).
