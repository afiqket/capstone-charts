Hovering over a dark blue country (one with a high number of psychiatrists per 100,000 people, e.g., Switzerland at ~47, France at 24, Norway at 23) triggers two effects defined in the `mousemove` handler on `.country` paths:

1. **Country outline highlights** — all countries' strokes reset to white at 0.4px, then the hovered country gets a dark stroke (`#333`) at 1.5px width and is raised to the top of the SVG so its border isn't clipped by neighbors.

2. **Tooltip appears** — a white rounded box (240×80, with drop shadow) is positioned near the cursor showing:
   - The country name in bold 15px black
   - The label "per 100,000 people" in small gray
   - The value formatted via `d3.format(".2~f")` in bold 22px dark blue (`#0868ac`) — e.g., hovering Switzerland shows "47", France shows "24"

   The tooltip flips to the left of the cursor if it would overflow the right edge, and is clamped vertically within the SVG.

On `mouseleave`, the stroke resets and the tooltip hides.
