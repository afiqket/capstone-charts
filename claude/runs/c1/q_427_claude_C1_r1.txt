**The 2010s (2010–2019)** had the highest average combined price index.

Since the index is dominated by Petroleum (the cereal crops are much smaller in magnitude), the decade ranking tracks Petroleum prices closely. Taking the per-decade average of each series and summing them:

| Decade | Petroleum avg | Wheat avg | Corn avg | **Combined** |
|--------|--------------:|----------:|---------:|-------------:|
| **2010s** | ~459.0 | ~29.4 | ~39.2 | **~527.6** |
| 1980s | ~384.6 | ~40.8 | ~53.5 | ~478.9 |
| 2000s | ~382.5 | ~24.5 | ~29.0 | ~436.0 |
| 1970s | ~267.4 | ~67.5 | ~91.7 | ~426.6 |

The 2010s edge out the 1980s and 2000s, driven mainly by very high petroleum prices (peaking at 628.7 in 2011 and staying above 580 through 2014). Note that the lone 2020 row doesn't form a full decade, so it isn't a competitor.
