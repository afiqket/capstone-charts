Summing the `count` column across all ages for each group:

The pattern in the data explains it: women dominate the younger ages (under ~32, since women tend to marry younger), but men outnumber women at essentially every age from the mid-30s upward. Those older age bands more than offset the female lead among young newlyweds, leaving the male total roughly 90,000 higher overall.
