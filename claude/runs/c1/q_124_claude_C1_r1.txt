Looking at the code:

- Its click handler is `playing ? pause() : play()` (`:300‑302`).

If pressed at the end of the timeline, it restarts from the beginning (`:441‑443`).

When pressed again to **pause** (`:471‑483`), it stops the timer and freezes the animation on the current date.
