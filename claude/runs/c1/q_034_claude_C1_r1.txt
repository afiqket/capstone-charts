**In the chart itself:** Click the **"Total Revenue"** tab at the top (the chart opens on "Total Bookings" by default). Then, on the left-hand **Mini** sub-chart, move your mouse over the data point sitting at **Jul 2025** on the x-axis. A tooltip will appear reading:

> **Mini**
> Month: Jul 2025
> Total Revenue: 7,266

This works because the code attaches `mouseover` handlers to each `circle`, and the tooltip prints `${pageData.yLabel}: ${d3.format(",")(d.value)}` — the exact aggregated value, not a rounded axis estimate.

**Why 7,266:** The chart computes each point by filtering the revenue CSV to `Vehicle Type (Calc) === "Mini"` and summing `Total Revenue` per `Month of Date`. The `Jul-25` Mini rows are:

| Total Revenue |
|---|
| 482 |
| 820 |
| 605 |
| 462 |
| 761 |
| 1964 |
| 1125 |
| 1047 |
| **Sum = 7,266** |

So the exact revenue value for Mini in July 2025 is **7,266** — readable directly from the hover tooltip on the Total Revenue page, and confirmed by summing the eight `Jul-25` Mini rows in the revenue data file.
