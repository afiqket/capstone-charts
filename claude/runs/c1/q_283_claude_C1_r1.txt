This chart is a **bar chart** of annual death rates by age group for the **United States**, with a year slider — it's not a line chart, and it doesn't include France.

If you hover near "France, 1900," nothing related to France will happen because:
- The data is filtered to `country = "United States"` only (line 13, 38).
- The visualization renders horizontal bars per age group, not a line chart.
- Tooltips only fire on `mousemove` over a `.bar` element (lines 121–126), showing the age group label and value formatted as `"{value} per 1,000"`, while dimming other bars to 0.5 opacity.

So hovering in empty space would do nothing. If you happened to be over one of the U.S. bars, you'd get that bar's tooltip — but there is no France series and no line chart in this code.
