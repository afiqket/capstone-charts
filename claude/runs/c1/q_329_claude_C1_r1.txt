Tokyo's population has followed a clear rise-peak-decline arc:

**Historical growth (1950–2020)** — nearly tripled:
- 1950: ~12.6 million
- 1970: ~21.7 million
- 1990: ~28.6 million
- 2010: ~32.6 million
- 2020: ~33.7 million (peak)

Growth was fastest in the postwar decades (gaining ~2–3 million every 5 years through the 1970s), then slowed steadily as it matured.

**Projected decline (2025–2100)** — a long, gradual fall:
- 2025: ~33.4 million
- 2050: ~30.7 million
- 2075: ~26.6 million
- 2100: ~24.1 million

By 2100, Tokyo is projected to lose roughly 9.6 million people from its 2020 peak, returning to a population level last seen around 1980. In the chart, the post-2020 trajectory is drawn as a dashed projection line to distinguish it from the solid historical estimates.
