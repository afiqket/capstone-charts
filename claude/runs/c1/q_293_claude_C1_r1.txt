From 1970 to 2021, Japan females show a clear upward trend in modal age at death — rising from 81.8 years in 1970 to 93.0 years in 2021, an increase of about 11 years. The growth is steady and consistent throughout the period, with only minor year-to-year fluctuations (e.g., a slight dip around 2010–2011 and from the 2020 peak of 93.1 to 93.0 in 2021).
