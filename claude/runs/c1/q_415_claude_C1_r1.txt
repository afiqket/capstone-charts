I computed Male − Female for each of the 48 year-rows for the United States, summed them, and divided by 48:

- Sum of differences ≈ 162.32
- Number of years = 48
- Average ≈ 162.32 / 48 ≈ **3.38**

Male values are consistently higher than female values throughout. The differences range from a low of ~1.5 (2012) to a high of 6 (1947), but cluster mostly around 3–4 across the series.
